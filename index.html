Cheesy Fried Eggs over Nacho Potato Hash

Roasted Southwest potatoes, peppers and onions topped with cheesy fried eggs, fresh pico de gallo and smoky red pepper crema.

2 servings425°F~30 min

Ingredients

  • Yukon Gold potatoes: 22 oz
  • Bell peppers: about 2
  • Red onions: about 2
  • Southwest spice blend: about 1.8 Tbsp
  • Eggs: about 4
  • Roma tomatoes: about 2
  • Cilantro: 0.5 oz
  • Jalapeños: about 2
  • Limes: about 2
  • Mexican cheese blend: about 0.9 cup
  • Smoky red pepper crema: about 3.7 Tbsp
  • Olive oil: about 7 tsp, plus a little for eggs
  • Sugar: 1/2 tsp
  • Salt & pepper: to taste

Directions

1

Prep

Preheat oven to 425°F. Dice potatoes and bell pepper into 1/2-inch pieces. Cut most of the onion into 1/2-inch wedges. Finely mince about 1 tablespoon onion and set aside.

2

Roast the hash

Toss potatoes, bell pepper and onion wedges with olive oil, Southwest spice, salt and pepper. Roast on the top rack until golden and tender, about 25 to 30 minutes.

3

Make pico

Dice tomato, chop cilantro and mince jalapeño. Combine tomato, half the cilantro, jalapeño and minced onion. Add juice from half a lime, 1/4 teaspoon sugar, salt and pepper.

4

Fry cheesy eggs

With about 5 minutes left on the vegetables, fry eggs in a little oil. Season with salt and pepper. During the final minute, sprinkle with Mexican cheese and cover briefly to melt.

5

Finish

Toss roasted vegetables with remaining lime juice to taste. Season again if needed.

6

Serve

Divide hash between plates and top with cheesy eggs, pico, smoky red pepper crema and remaining cilantro.

Black Bean & Green Pepper Flautas

Crispy pan-fried flautas filled with seasoned black beans, green pepper, onion and pepper jack, served with pico, guacamole and sour cream.

2 servingsVegetarian

Ingredients

  • Yellow onions: about 3
  • Long green peppers: about 3
  • Black beans: about 3 cans
  • Tomatoes: about 3
  • Scallions: about 5
  • Limes: about 3
  • Southwest spice: about 2.7 Tbsp
  • Flour tortillas: about 16
  • Pepper jack: about 1.4 cups
  • Guacamole: about 10.8 Tbsp
  • Sour cream: about 8.1 Tbsp
  • Butter: about 2.7 Tbsp
  • Oil, salt & pepper: as needed

Directions

1

Prep and make pico

Dice tomato, slice scallions, halve lime, slice onion and dice green pepper. Drain beans and reserve the liquid. Combine tomato, scallions, lime juice and a drizzle of olive oil. Season.

2

Cook filling

Sauté onion and green pepper in oil over medium-high heat for 5 to 6 minutes. Add Southwest spice and half the beans. Cook 2 to 3 minutes, season and remove from heat.

3

Mash beans

Cook remaining beans in a small pot for 2 to 3 minutes. Add 1/4 cup reserved bean liquid and simmer 1 to 2 minutes. Stir in 1 tablespoon butter, remove from heat, mash mostly smooth and season.

4

Roll flautas

Spread tortillas with mashed beans. Add a small amount of filling and pepper jack. Roll tightly from the filled side and place seam-side down.

5

Pan-fry

Heat a generous drizzle of oil. Cook flautas seam-side down, turning carefully, until golden and crispy, about 1 to 2 minutes per side.

6

Serve

Top with guacamole, pico and sour cream, or serve toppings on the side. Add lime wedges.

BBQ Dry-Rub Tofu

BBQ-seasoned tofu with roasted potatoes, buttery corn and ranch-green onion topping.

2 servingsVegetarian~35 min

Ingredients

  • Tofu: about 2 blocks
  • BBQ seasoning: about 1.9 Tbsp
  • Corn kernels: 213.9 g
  • Ranch dressing: 7.6 Tbsp
  • Yellow potatoes: 662.4 g
  • Garlic salt: 1.9 tsp
  • Green onions: about 2
  • Unsalted butter: 1.9 Tbsp
  • Oil: 3.8 Tbsp
  • Salt & pepper: to taste

Directions

1

Roast potatoes

Cut potatoes into 1/4-inch rounds. Toss with 1 tablespoon oil, about 1/4 teaspoon garlic salt and pepper. Roast on the top rack until tender, 24 to 25 minutes.

2

Roast tofu

Pat tofu dry and cut each block in half horizontally into steaks. Poke with a fork. Toss with BBQ seasoning, another 1/4 teaspoon garlic salt and 1 tablespoon oil. Roast on a second sheet, flipping halfway, 8 to 10 minutes.

3

Cook corn

Melt 1 tablespoon butter in a nonstick pan. Add corn and 2 tablespoons water. Cover and cook 5 to 6 minutes, stirring occasionally. Season.

4

Make ranch topping

Thinly slice green onion. Mix ranch with half the green onion and season to taste.

5

Serve

Slice tofu and divide tofu, corn and potatoes between plates. Drizzle ranch over corn, sprinkle with remaining green onion and serve extra ranch on the side.

Cajun Sea Bass and Parsley Dressing

Cajun-spiced sea bass with herby parsley dressing, mashed potatoes, green beans and Tenderstem broccoli.

3 servings20 minSeafood

Ingredients

  • Potatoes: 1,214.1 g
  • Flat-leaf parsley: about 3 handfuls/bunches
  • Green beans: 215.8 g
  • Garlic cloves: about 3
  • Lemon: about 1 to 2
  • Cajun spice mix: about 2.7 Tbsp
  • Sea bass fillets: about 5 to 6
  • Tenderstem broccoli: 215.8 g
  • Olive oil for dressing: about 4 Tbsp
  • Milk & butter: optional for mash
  • Salt, pepper & cooking oil: as needed

Directions

1

Boil potatoes

Bring salted water to a boil. Cut potatoes into 2 cm chunks and cook until easily pierced with a knife, 15 to 18 minutes.

2

Prep

Roughly chop parsley including stalks. Trim green beans. Grate garlic. Zest and halve lemon.

3

Season fish

Rub sea bass with half the Cajun spice, salt, pepper and a drizzle of oil. Preheat grill to medium-high.

4

Make dressing

Mix remaining Cajun spice with lemon zest, some lemon juice, salt, half the parsley and olive oil. Add more lemon juice to taste.

5

Cook fish and vegetables

Place sea bass skin-side up on a foil-lined tray and grill 5 to 6 minutes until opaque and crisp-skinned. Meanwhile stir-fry green beans and broccoli for 2 to 3 minutes, add garlic for 1 minute, then add a splash of water, cover and cook 2 to 3 minutes more.

6

Mash and serve

Drain potatoes, add a splash of milk and butter if desired, and mash smooth. Stir in remaining parsley and season. Plate mash with vegetables and sea bass. Spoon parsley dressing over the top.
