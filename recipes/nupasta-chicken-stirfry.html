NuPasta Chicken Stirfry

Chicken, vegetables and NuPasta tossed with a simple pasta sauce.

1 small serving20 minGluten-free33g protein

Ingredients

  • NuPasta: 1 package (35 calories)
  • Pasta sauce: 1/2 cup (up to 60 calories)
  • Chicken breast: 90g
  • Vegetables: 1/2 serving
  • Spices/condiments: to taste
  • Cooking spray

Directions

1

Cook NuPasta according to package instructions and set aside.

2

Heat a pan over medium heat and coat with cooking spray. Add vegetables and chicken and sauté until chicken is cooked through. Season to taste.

3

Add cooked NuPasta and pasta sauce. Sauté together for a few minutes.

4

Serve together in a bowl.

Nutrition

Small: 320 calories · 6g fat · 33g carbs · 15g fiber · 33g protein.

Large version: use 3/4 cup sauce, 180g chicken breast and 1 full serving vegetables. About 555 calories · 9g fat · 48g carbs · 22g fiber · 65g protein.
