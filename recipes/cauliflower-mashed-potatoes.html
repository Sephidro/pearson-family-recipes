Cauliflower Mashed Potatoes

Potatoes blended with cauliflower for a lighter, higher-fiber mash.

8 servings20 minVegetarianGluten-free

Ingredients

  • Potatoes: 2 lb
  • Cauliflower florets: 2 lb
  • Fat-free sour cream: 1 cup
  • Guar gum or xanthan gum: 3 tsp
  • Baking powder: 2 tsp
  • Spices: to taste

Directions

1

Bring salted water to a boil. Reduce to medium, add potatoes and cook until fully tender. Drain.

2

Cook cauliflower in boiling water until tender, then drain.

3

Blend potatoes, cauliflower, baking powder, spices, 1/2 cup sour cream and guar or xanthan gum until smooth.

4

Serve with the remaining fat-free sour cream and preferred spices.

Nutrition per serving

150 calories · 0g fat · 33g carbs · 8g fiber · 5g protein.

Lite version: use 1 lb potatoes, 1 lb cauliflower, 2 tsp guar/xanthan gum and add 2 tsp baking powder. About 90 calories · 19g carbs · 4g fiber · 3g protein.
