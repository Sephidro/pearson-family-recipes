Thai Green Style Diced Chicken Breast Curry

Quick Thai-style green chicken curry with coconut milk, spinach, chilli, lime and jasmine rice.

2 servings20 minEasyChicken

Ingredients

  • Jasmine rice: 150 g
  • Garlic cloves: 2
  • Thai green curry paste: 45 g
  • Coconut milk: 200 ml
  • Diced chicken breast: 260 g
  • Chicken stock paste: 10 g
  • Lime: 1
  • Red chilli: 1/2
  • Spring onion: 1
  • Baby spinach: amount not visible in screenshot
  • Salt, pepper and cooking oil: as needed
  • Water for sauce: use the amount from your original recipe/app if shown elsewhere

Directions

1

Cook the rice

Boil a full kettle. Pour the boiling water into a large saucepan with about 1/4 teaspoon salt over high heat. Add the jasmine rice and cook for 10 to 12 minutes. Drain in a sieve, return to the pan, cover with a lid and set aside until serving.

2

Start the curry sauce

Peel and grate the garlic, or use a garlic press. Heat a drizzle of oil in a large saucepan over medium-high heat. Add the Thai green curry paste and garlic and stir-fry until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Stir in the coconut milk, chicken stock paste and the recipe's water for the sauce.

3

Cook the chicken

Stir in the diced chicken and bring the curry to a boil, then lower the heat. Simmer until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce has thickened, about 10 to 12 minutes. The chicken should no longer be pink in the middle.

4

Prep the garnish

Meanwhile, halve the chilli lengthwise, remove the seeds if you want less heat, then thinly slice. Trim and thinly slice the spring onion. Zest the lime, then cut it in half.

5

Finish the curry

Once the chicken is cooked and the sauce has thickened, add the spinach a handful at a time until wilted and piping hot, about 1 to 2 minutes. Squeeze in some lime juice. Taste and season with salt, pepper and more lime juice if needed, then remove from the heat.

6

Serve

Fluff the rice with a fork and stir through the lime zest. Divide the zesty rice between bowls and spoon the chicken curry over the top. Sprinkle with sliced chilli and spring onion. Cut any remaining lime into wedges and serve alongside.
