Thai Green Style Diced Chicken Breast Curry

Thai green chicken curry with spinach, vegetables, lime and jasmine rice.

Ingredients

  • Jasmine rice: 100 g dry
  • Garlic cloves: 2
  • Thai green curry paste: 45 g
  • Light coconut milk: 200 ml
  • Diced chicken breast: 320 g
  • Chicken stock paste: 10 g
  • Baby spinach: 150 g
  • Bell pepper or broccoli: 250 g
  • Lime: 1
  • Red chilli: 1/2
  • Spring onion: 1
  • Cooking oil: 1 tsp
  • Salt and pepper: to taste

Directions

1

Cook the rice

Cook the jasmine rice in boiling salted water for 10 to 12 minutes. Drain, cover and set aside.

2

Start the curry

Grate the garlic. Heat 1 teaspoon oil in a large saucepan over medium-high heat. Add the curry paste and garlic and cook for about 30 seconds. Stir in the light coconut milk, chicken stock paste and enough water to loosen the sauce.

3

Cook chicken and vegetables

Add the chicken and bell pepper or broccoli. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce has thickened, about 10 to 12 minutes.

4

Prep garnish

Thinly slice the chilli and spring onion. Zest and halve the lime.

5

Finish

Add spinach a handful at a time until wilted, about 1 to 2 minutes. Add lime juice and season with salt and pepper.

6

Serve

Stir lime zest through the rice. Divide rice between bowls, spoon over the chicken curry and top with chilli and spring onion. Serve lime wedges alongside.
