Chicken & Pepper Stromboli

Chicken, peppers, mozzarella and marinara rolled into a crisp whole-wheat stromboli.

2 servings~30 minMediumChicken

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast strips: 12 oz
  • Bell peppers: 2
  • Garlic: 1 clove
  • Whole-wheat pizza dough: 6 to 7 oz
  • Reduced-fat cream cheese: 1 Tbsp
  • Part-skim mozzarella: 1/3 cup
  • Tuscan heat spice: 1 Tbsp
  • Marinara: 5 oz, preferably no-added-sugar
  • Olive oil: 2 tsp total
  • Salt and pepper: to taste

Directions

1

Prep

Preheat oven to 425°F. Thinly slice the bell peppers. Mince the garlic. Pat the chicken dry and season lightly with salt and pepper.

2

Cook chicken and peppers

Heat 1 teaspoon olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add peppers and cook until tender, about 4 to 6 minutes. Add chicken and cook until browned and cooked through, about 4 to 6 minutes more.

3

Finish the filling

Stir in the cream cheese, 1 teaspoon Tuscan heat spice and 1/4 cup water. Cook until most of the water has evaporated, about 1 to 3 minutes. Remove from heat.

4

Shape the dough

Divide the pizza dough into two pieces and stretch each into roughly a 6-by-8-inch rectangle on a lightly oiled or parchment-lined baking sheet.

5

Fill and roll

Sprinkle mozzarella over the dough, leaving about a 1/2-inch border. Add the chicken-and-pepper filling. Roll each rectangle up from a short side to form a log. Place seam-side down, pinch the ends closed and tuck them underneath.

6

Season and bake

Mix the minced garlic with the remaining 1 teaspoon olive oil and remaining Tuscan heat spice. Brush over the stromboli. Cut three shallow diagonal slits across the top of each log. Bake on the middle rack until golden and crisp, about 14 to 16 minutes. Chicken should reach 165°F.

7

Serve

Warm the marinara while the stromboli finishes. Let the stromboli rest briefly, then slice crosswise and serve with warm marinara for dipping.
