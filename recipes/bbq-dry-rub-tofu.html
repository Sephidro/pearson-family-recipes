BBQ Dry-Rub Tofu

BBQ tofu with roasted potatoes, corn, green vegetables and Greek-yogurt ranch.

Ingredients

  • Extra-firm tofu: 2 blocks
  • BBQ seasoning: 1 1/2 Tbsp
  • Corn kernels: 200 g
  • Yellow potatoes: 400 g
  • Broccoli or green beans: 300 g
  • Green onions: 2
  • Nonfat Greek yogurt: 3/4 cup
  • Ranch seasoning: to taste
  • Garlic salt: 1 tsp
  • Olive oil: 1 1/2 Tbsp total
  • Salt and pepper: to taste

Directions

1

Cut potatoes into rounds. Toss with 2 teaspoons olive oil, garlic salt and pepper. Roast at 425°F for about 24 to 25 minutes.

2

Pat tofu dry, cut into steaks and season with BBQ seasoning. Brush with 2 teaspoons olive oil. Roast beside the potatoes or on a second sheet, flipping halfway, until browned.

3

Cook corn and broccoli or green beans in a nonstick pan with a splash of water. Add 1 teaspoon olive oil near the end for flavor, then season.

4

Mix Greek yogurt with ranch seasoning and half the sliced green onion. Thin with a spoonful of water if needed.

5

Divide tofu, potatoes, corn and vegetables between plates. Spoon the yogurt ranch over the vegetables and finish with remaining green onion.
