BBQ Dry-Rub Tofu

BBQ-seasoned tofu with roasted potatoes, buttery corn and ranch-green onion topping.

Ingredients

  • Tofu: about 2 blocks
  • BBQ seasoning: about 1.9 Tbsp
  • Corn kernels: 213.9 g
  • Ranch dressing: 7.6 Tbsp
  • Yellow potatoes: 662.4 g
  • Garlic salt: 1.9 tsp
  • Green onions: about 2
  • Unsalted butter: 1.9 Tbsp
  • Oil: 3.8 Tbsp
  • Salt and pepper: to taste

Directions

1

Cut potatoes into 1/4-inch rounds. Toss with 1 tablespoon oil, garlic salt and pepper. Roast on the top rack 24 to 25 minutes.

2

Pat tofu dry, cut into flat steaks, poke with a fork, and coat with BBQ seasoning, garlic salt and oil. Roast on a second sheet, flipping halfway, 8 to 10 minutes.

3

Melt butter in a nonstick pan. Add corn and 2 tablespoons water. Cover and cook 5 to 6 minutes. Season.

4

Thinly slice green onion. Mix ranch with half the green onion and season.

5

Slice tofu and serve with corn and potatoes. Drizzle ranch over corn and top with remaining green onion.
