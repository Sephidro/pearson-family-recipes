Black Bean & Green Pepper Flautas

Baked bean-and-pepper flautas with pico, guacamole and Greek yogurt.

Ingredients

  • Yellow onion: 1 large
  • Long green peppers: 2
  • Black beans: 2 cans, drained
  • Roma tomatoes: 2
  • Scallions: 3
  • Limes: 2
  • Southwest spice: 1 Tbsp
  • High-fiber flour tortillas: 8
  • Reduced-fat pepper jack: 1 cup
  • Guacamole: 1/2 cup
  • Nonfat Greek yogurt: 1 cup
  • Olive oil: 2 tsp
  • Salt and pepper: to taste

Directions

1

Dice tomato, slice scallions, halve lime, slice onion and dice peppers. Make pico with tomato, scallions, lime juice, salt and pepper.

2

Sauté onion and peppers in 1 teaspoon olive oil for 5 to 6 minutes. Add Southwest spice and 1 can of beans. Cook 2 to 3 minutes.

3

Warm the remaining beans with a splash of water, then mash mostly smooth. Season to taste.

4

Spread each tortilla with mashed beans. Add pepper-and-bean filling and pepper jack. Roll tightly and place seam-side down on a lined baking sheet.

5

Brush or mist lightly with the remaining olive oil. Bake at 425°F until crisp and browned, about 12 to 16 minutes, turning once if needed.

6

Serve with pico, guacamole, Greek yogurt and lime.
