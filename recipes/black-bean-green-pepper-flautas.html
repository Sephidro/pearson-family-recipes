Black Bean & Green Pepper Flautas

Crispy flautas filled with seasoned black beans, green pepper, onion and pepper jack.

Ingredients

  • Yellow onions: about 3
  • Long green peppers: about 3
  • Black beans: about 3 cans
  • Tomatoes: about 3
  • Scallions: about 5
  • Limes: about 3
  • Southwest spice: about 2.7 Tbsp
  • Flour tortillas: about 16
  • Pepper jack: about 1.4 cups
  • Guacamole: about 10.8 Tbsp
  • Sour cream: about 8.1 Tbsp
  • Butter: about 2.7 Tbsp
  • Oil, salt and pepper: as needed

Directions

1

Prep tomato, scallions, lime, onion and green pepper. Drain beans and reserve liquid. Make pico with tomato, scallions, lime juice, olive oil, salt and pepper.

2

Sauté onion and green pepper 5 to 6 minutes. Add Southwest spice and half the beans. Cook 2 to 3 minutes and season.

3

Cook remaining beans 2 to 3 minutes. Add 1/4 cup reserved bean liquid, simmer 1 to 2 minutes, stir in 1 tablespoon butter and mash mostly smooth.

4

Spread tortillas with mashed beans. Add filling and pepper jack. Roll tightly and place seam-side down.

5

Pan-fry in oil until golden and crisp, about 1 to 2 minutes per side.

6

Serve with guacamole, pico, sour cream and lime wedges.
