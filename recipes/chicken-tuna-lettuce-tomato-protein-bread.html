Chicken/Tuna, Lettuce & Tomato on Protein Bread

A three-minute high-protein sandwich with chicken or tuna.

1 sandwich3 min45g protein

Ingredients

  • Protein bread: 2 slices
  • Tomato: sliced, up to 20 calories
  • Lettuce: up to 10 calories
  • Chicken: 4 thin slices, or water-packed tuna: 1/2 can
  • Light mayonnaise: 1 Tbsp

Directions

Spread the light mayonnaise on the bread, then layer with lettuce, tomato and chicken or drained tuna. Close the sandwich and serve.

Nutrition

ICON protein bread: 440 calories · 12g fat · 38g carbs · 5g fiber · 45g protein.

P28 bread: 380 calories · 9g fat · 32g carbs · 5g fiber · 43g protein.

Ezekiel 4:9 bread: 280 calories · 3g fat · 38g carbs · 7g fiber · 25g protein.

Regular white bread: 275 calories · 4g fat · 38g carbs · 2g fiber · 25g protein.
