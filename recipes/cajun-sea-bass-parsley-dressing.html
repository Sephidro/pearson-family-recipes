Cajun Sea Bass and Parsley Dressing

Sea bass with parsley dressing, mashed potatoes, green beans and Tenderstem broccoli.

Ingredients (3 servings)

  • Potatoes: 600 g
  • Flat-leaf parsley: 1 large bunch
  • Green beans: 300 g
  • Tenderstem broccoli: 300 g
  • Garlic cloves: 3
  • Lemon: 1 to 2
  • Cajun spice mix: 2 Tbsp
  • Sea bass fillets: 5 to 6
  • Olive oil for dressing: 2 Tbsp
  • Nonfat Greek yogurt or skim milk: small amount for mash
  • Salt, pepper and a small amount of cooking oil: as needed

Directions

1

Boil potato chunks in salted water until easily pierced, 15 to 18 minutes.

2

Chop parsley, trim green beans, grate garlic, and zest and halve the lemon.

3

Rub sea bass with half the Cajun spice, salt, pepper and a very light coating of oil. Preheat grill to medium-high.

4

Mix remaining Cajun spice with lemon zest, lemon juice, half the parsley and 2 tablespoons olive oil. Add water or extra lemon juice if you want a thinner dressing.

5

Grill sea bass skin-side up 5 to 6 minutes. Stir-fry beans and broccoli in a nonstick pan with a light amount of oil, add garlic for 1 minute, then add a splash of water, cover and cook until tender.

6

Drain and mash potatoes with a spoonful of Greek yogurt or a splash of skim milk. Stir in remaining parsley. Divide mash, vegetables and fish between plates and spoon over parsley dressing.
