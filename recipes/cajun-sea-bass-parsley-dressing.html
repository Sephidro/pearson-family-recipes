Cajun Sea Bass and Parsley Dressing

Cajun sea bass with parsley dressing, mash, green beans and Tenderstem broccoli.

Ingredients

  • Potatoes: 1,214.1 g
  • Flat-leaf parsley: about 3 handfuls/bunches
  • Green beans: 215.8 g
  • Garlic cloves: about 3
  • Lemon: about 1 to 2
  • Cajun spice mix: about 2.7 Tbsp
  • Sea bass fillets: about 5 to 6
  • Tenderstem broccoli: 215.8 g
  • Olive oil for dressing: about 4 Tbsp
  • Milk and butter: optional for mash
  • Salt, pepper and cooking oil: as needed

Directions

1

Boil potato chunks in salted water until easily pierced, 15 to 18 minutes.

2

Chop parsley, trim green beans, grate garlic, and zest and halve lemon.

3

Rub sea bass with half the Cajun spice, salt, pepper and a drizzle of oil. Preheat grill to medium-high.

4

Mix remaining Cajun spice with lemon zest, lemon juice, salt, half the parsley and olive oil.

5

Grill sea bass skin-side up 5 to 6 minutes. Stir-fry beans and broccoli 2 to 3 minutes, add garlic for 1 minute, then add a splash of water, cover and cook 2 to 3 minutes more.

6

Drain and mash potatoes with optional milk and butter. Stir in remaining parsley. Serve mash with vegetables and fish, topped with parsley dressing.
