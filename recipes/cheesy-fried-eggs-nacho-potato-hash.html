Cheesy Fried Eggs over Nacho Potato Hash

Roasted Southwest potatoes, peppers and onions topped with cheesy eggs, pico and a smoky yogurt crema.

2 servings425°F~30 min

Ingredients

  • Yukon Gold potatoes: 12 oz
  • Bell peppers: 2
  • Red onion: 1 medium
  • Southwest spice: 1 Tbsp
  • Whole eggs: 4
  • Liquid egg whites: 400 g
  • Roma tomatoes: 2
  • Cilantro: 0.5 oz
  • Jalapeño: 1
  • Lime: 1
  • Reduced-fat Mexican cheese: 1/2 cup
  • Nonfat Greek yogurt: 1/3 cup
  • Smoked paprika or hot sauce: to taste
  • Olive oil: 2 tsp
  • Salt & pepper: to taste

Directions

1

Prep

Preheat oven to 425°F. Dice potatoes and peppers into 1/2-inch pieces. Cut most of the onion into wedges and finely mince a small amount for the pico.

2

Roast

Toss potatoes, peppers and onion wedges with 2 teaspoons olive oil, Southwest spice, salt and pepper. Roast 25 to 30 minutes until browned and tender.

3

Make pico

Combine diced tomato, cilantro, jalapeño and minced onion with lime juice, salt and pepper.

4

Cook the eggs

Whisk the whole eggs and egg whites together. Cook in a nonstick pan until nearly set, then add the reduced-fat cheese and finish cooking.

5

Make the crema

Mix Greek yogurt with smoked paprika or hot sauce, a squeeze of lime and a pinch of salt.

6

Serve

Divide the roasted hash between plates. Top with cheesy eggs, pico and the yogurt crema.
