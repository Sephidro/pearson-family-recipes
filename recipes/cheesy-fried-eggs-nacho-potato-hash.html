Cheesy Fried Eggs over Nacho Potato Hash

Roasted Southwest potatoes, peppers and onions topped with cheesy fried eggs, pico and smoky red pepper crema.

2 servings425°F~30 min

Ingredients

  • Yukon Gold potatoes: 22 oz
  • Bell peppers: about 2
  • Red onions: about 2
  • Southwest spice: about 1.8 Tbsp
  • Eggs: about 4
  • Roma tomatoes: about 2
  • Cilantro: 0.5 oz
  • Jalapeños: about 2
  • Limes: about 2
  • Mexican cheese: about 0.9 cup
  • Smoky red pepper crema: about 3.7 Tbsp
  • Olive oil: about 7 tsp
  • Sugar: 1/2 tsp
  • Salt & pepper: to taste

Directions

1

Prep

Preheat oven to 425°F. Dice potatoes and bell pepper into 1/2-inch pieces. Cut most onion into wedges. Finely mince about 1 tablespoon onion and set aside.

2

Roast

Toss potatoes, bell pepper and onion wedges with olive oil, Southwest spice, salt and pepper. Roast 25 to 30 minutes until golden and tender.

3

Make pico

Combine diced tomato, half the cilantro, jalapeño and minced onion. Add juice from half a lime, sugar, salt and pepper.

4

Cook eggs

Fry eggs in a little oil. During the final minute, top with Mexican cheese and cover briefly to melt.

5

Finish

Toss roasted vegetables with remaining lime juice and season to taste.

6

Serve

Top hash with cheesy eggs, pico, smoky red pepper crema and remaining cilantro.
