Wide Awake Smoothie

Banana, berries, apple and spinach blended with plant-based milk.

Ingredients

  • Strawberries: 146.5 g
  • Apple: 219.7 g
  • Raspberries: 146.5 g
  • Spinach: 87.9 g
  • Banana: 345.6 g
  • Plant-based milk: 2.9 cups
  • Chia seeds: optional
  • Coconut: optional

Directions

1

Add strawberries, apple, raspberries, spinach, banana and plant-based milk to a blender. Blend until smooth.

2

Pour into glasses and top with chia seeds and coconut if desired.
