Wide Awake Smoothie

A high-protein berry-and-spinach smoothie with a much smaller fruit sugar load than the original.

Goal-aligned changes

Most of the banana and apple are removed. Protein, fiber and unsaturated fats go up so this works better as a meal instead of a large blended-fruit drink.

Ingredients (2 servings)

  • Strawberries: 1 cup
  • Raspberries: 1 cup
  • Banana: 1/2 medium
  • Spinach: 2 packed cups
  • Unsweetened soy milk: 2 cups
  • Nonfat Greek yogurt: 1 cup
  • Protein powder: 2 scoops
  • Chia seeds or ground flax: 2 Tbsp
  • Ice and water: as needed
  • Cinnamon: optional

Directions

1

Add everything to a blender and blend until completely smooth. Add water or ice to reach your preferred thickness.

2

Divide into two glasses. Skip sweetened coconut or added honey/syrup.
