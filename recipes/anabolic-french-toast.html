Anabolic French Toast

High-protein French toast with blueberries and low-calorie syrup.

2 servings15 minVegetarian19g protein

Ingredients

  • Egg whites: 1 cup
  • White bread: 4 slices (about 80 calories each)
  • Sweetener: 1 packet
  • Cinnamon: 1 tsp
  • Vanilla extract: 1 Tbsp
  • Blueberries: 1/2 cup
  • Low-calorie syrup: 1/2 cup
  • Cooking spray

Directions

1

Whisk egg whites, sweetener, cinnamon and vanilla until evenly combined.

2

Heat a griddle over low-medium heat and coat with cooking spray.

3

Dip bread slices into the egg-white mixture and transfer to the pan. Spoon any remaining mixture into the bread so it absorbs as much as possible.

4

Cook about 3 to 4 minutes per side.

5

Serve topped with blueberries and low-calorie syrup.

Nutrition per serving

250 calories · 3g fat · 36g carbs · 2.5g fiber · 19g protein

P28 bread version: 350 calories · 8g fat · 31g carbs · 5g fiber · 39g protein.

ICON protein bread version: 370 calories · 11g fat · 36g carbs · 8g fiber · 43g protein.
