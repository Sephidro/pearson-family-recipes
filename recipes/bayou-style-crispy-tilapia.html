Bayou-Style Crispy Tilapia

Crispy panko-coated tilapia with paprika potato wedges, a tomato-and-greens salad, and creamy remoulade.

2 servings425°FMediumSeafood

Ingredients

  • Yukon Gold potatoes: 20 oz
  • Lemon: about 2
  • Paprika: about 1.7 tsp
  • Dijon mustard: about 3.3 tsp
  • Cornstarch: about 1.7 Tbsp
  • Fry seasoning: about 1.7 Tbsp
  • Scallions: about 3
  • Mixed greens: 3.3 oz
  • Sour cream: about 6.7 Tbsp
  • Mayonnaise: about 3.3 Tbsp
  • Panko breadcrumbs: about 0.8 cup
  • Tilapia: 18.3 oz
  • Roma tomatoes: about 2
  • Italian dressing: 2.5 oz
  • Olive oil: about 1.7 tsp
  • Vegetable oil: enough for shallow frying
  • Salt and pepper: to taste

Directions

1

Prep and make the remoulade

Preheat oven to 425°F. Wash and dry the produce. Trim and thinly slice the scallions, separating whites from greens. Finely chop enough scallion whites to make about 1 tablespoon. Quarter the lemon. Cut potatoes into 1/2-inch wedges. In a small bowl, combine mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, chopped scallion whites, half the sour cream, half the paprika, and a squeeze of lemon juice. Season with salt and pepper.

2

Roast the potatoes

Toss potatoes on a baking sheet with a drizzle of olive oil, the remaining paprika, salt, and pepper. Roast on the top rack until golden brown and crispy, about 20 to 25 minutes.

3

Bread the tilapia

Halve the tilapia fillets lengthwise so each fillet makes one larger and one smaller piece. Pat dry and season with salt and pepper. In a shallow dish, combine fry seasoning, the remaining sour cream, and 1 tablespoon water. On a plate, combine panko and cornstarch and season with salt. Dip each fish piece into the sour cream mixture, then press into the panko mixture until evenly coated.

4

Fry until crisp

Heat about a 1/3-inch layer of vegetable oil in a large nonstick pan over medium-high heat. When a pinch of panko sizzles immediately, add the tilapia. Cook until the crust is golden and the fish is cooked through, about 2 to 3 minutes per side. Smaller pieces may finish sooner. Transfer to a paper-towel-lined plate and season lightly with salt.

5

Make the salad

While the fish cooks, halve the tomato lengthwise and thinly slice crosswise into half-moons. Toss with mixed greens and Italian dressing in a large bowl.

6

Serve

Divide tilapia, salad, and potato wedges between plates. Sprinkle with scallion greens. Serve with the remoulade and remaining lemon wedges on the side.
