Bayou-Style Crispy Tilapia

Oven-crisp panko tilapia with a larger salad, smaller potato portion and lighter remoulade.

Goal-aligned changes

The tilapia stays. Shallow frying becomes baking or air-frying, potatoes come down, salad goes up, and the sauce becomes mostly Greek yogurt.

Ingredients

  • Yukon Gold potatoes: 12 oz
  • Lemon: 1
  • Paprika: 1 tsp
  • Dijon mustard: 2 tsp
  • Cornstarch: 1 Tbsp
  • Fry seasoning: 1 Tbsp
  • Scallions: 3
  • Mixed greens: 6 oz
  • Nonfat Greek yogurt: 3/4 cup
  • Light mayonnaise: 1 Tbsp
  • Panko breadcrumbs: 1/2 cup
  • Tilapia: 18 oz
  • Roma tomatoes: 2
  • Italian dressing: 2 Tbsp
  • Olive oil: 2 tsp total
  • Salt and pepper: to taste

Directions

1

Prep and make remoulade

Preheat oven to 425°F. Slice scallions and quarter the lemon. Cut potatoes into 1/2-inch wedges. Mix Greek yogurt, light mayo, Dijon, scallion whites, half the paprika and lemon juice. Season with salt and pepper.

2

Roast the potatoes

Toss potatoes with 1 teaspoon olive oil, remaining paprika, salt and pepper. Roast until browned and crisp, 20 to 25 minutes.

3

Bread the fish

Pat tilapia dry and season. Mix fry seasoning with a few spoonfuls of Greek yogurt and a splash of water. Mix panko and cornstarch separately. Coat the fish in the yogurt mixture, then press into the panko.

4

Bake or air-fry

Place fish on a lined rack or baking sheet, mist or brush with the remaining teaspoon of olive oil and bake until crisp and cooked through, about 10 to 14 minutes depending on thickness. An air fryer also works well.

5

Make the salad

Slice tomatoes and toss with mixed greens and 2 tablespoons Italian dressing.

6

Serve

Divide fish, salad and potato wedges between plates. Add scallion greens and serve the lighter remoulade and lemon on the side.
