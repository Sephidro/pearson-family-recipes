Ciao Carb Proto Pasta with Beef Tomato Sauce

High-protein pasta with extra-lean beef, vegetables and tomato-basil sauce.

1 small serving20 min49g protein11g fiber

Ingredients

  • Ciao Carb Proto Pasta: 1/2 package (50g), or equivalent up to 170 calories
  • Tomato & basil pasta sauce: 1/4 cup, up to 30 calories
  • Extra-lean ground beef: 50g
  • Vegetables: 1/2 serving, up to 50 calories
  • Spices/condiments: to taste, up to 10 calories
  • Cooking spray

Directions

1

Boil pasta according to package instructions. Strain and rinse under cold water, then set aside.

2

Heat a griddle or pan over low-medium heat and coat with cooking spray. Add ground beef and sauté until cooked through. Season to taste.

3

Heat the tomato sauce and vegetables.

4

Combine pasta, beef, vegetables and sauce and serve.

Nutrition

Small: 370 calories · 8g fat · 25g carbs · 11g fiber · 49g protein.

Large version: 1 package pasta, 1/2 cup sauce, 70g extra-lean beef and 1/2 serving vegetables. About 600 calories · 11g fat · 40g carbs · 17g fiber · 84g protein.
